## How to use this brief

This brief connects the copy work to the real sites. It holds the decisions, the status of each page, the open questions and the build tasks for every site, so each can be worked on without reopening the whole conversation. The full draft copy lives in the working doc, "Copy system: Mischa, Fractional by Nature, HelloMaker".

**From Claude Code, in each repo.**

1. Export or copy this doc and the working doc as Markdown.
2. Save them in the repo as `docs/copy-brief.md` and `docs/copy-drafts.md`.
3. Add one line to the repo's `CLAUDE.md`: "Before changing copy, navigation or metadata, read docs/copy-brief.md (the shared sections and this site's section) and docs/copy-drafts.md."
4. Ask Claude Code to work one page at a time, starting with anything labelled Final or Draft.

**Status labels.**

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Final | Use as written |
| Draft | Use it, and flag anything that looks off |
| Needs input | Has a [bracket] or an open question. Do not invent the answer or publish the placeholder |
| Not started | No copy yet |

**Rules for whoever builds.**

- Never invent facts about Mischa, his family or his clients. If a fact is missing, leave the bracket and list it as a question.
- No em dashes in drafted writing. Mischa finds they read as AI-edited.
- Keep his kids' faces out of share images and anything that will be widely re-shared.
- Do not link to Fractional by Nature until it is live.
- Where a decision is recorded in the log below, follow it. If the code disagrees, ask first.

## Shared foundation

These ideas have underpinned his life and work for years, and his next decade will be focused on addressing them. His response has three parts: purposeful organisations (Fractional by Nature), young people and learning (HelloMaker, Open Campus), and a home life that fits the working life (unschooling, four working days, off-grid time).

**Goals, public version.** Enough income to live well, four working days a week, and work his kids would be proud of. Money specifics are deliberately not in this brief.
