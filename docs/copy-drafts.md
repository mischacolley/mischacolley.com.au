We're asking more of the planet than it can give, when a good economy would meet everyone's needs within its limits. Each is what happens when relationships, with people, with place, with our own work, get treated as something to optimise away.

**Why it's personal.** It is why you unschool your kids, why relationships sit at the centre of how you work, and why your kids are the filter for every project you take on.
