# mischacolley.com.au

## Copy, navigation and metadata

Before changing any copy, navigation or metadata, read `docs/copy-brief.md`
(the shared sections and this site's section), `docs/copy-drafts.md` and
`docs/story-map.md`.

`story-map.md` is the one to read first: it records what is in the code today,
why it is changing, and what each change adds to the overall story. It also
lists the known hazards and the questions still waiting on Mischa. Update it in
the same commit as the change it describes.

Two standing rules from the brief, worth repeating here:

- Never invent facts about Mischa, his family or his clients. If a fact is
  missing, leave the bracket and list it as a question.
- No em dashes in drafted copy. They read as AI-edited.
